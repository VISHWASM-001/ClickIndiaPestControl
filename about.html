Our Story

From a Family Garage to a Trusted Name

Click India Pest Control was founded by Honnayya P with a simple vision: to provide safe, effective, and reliable pest control solutions for homes and businesses. We believe pest control is not just about treating pests, but about understanding the problem and choosing the right solution.

Our approach is supported by scientific expertise and practical experience. Prof. B. E. Kumara Swamy, Founder’s Mentor and Scientific Advisor at Kuvempu University, provides valuable scientific guidance, while Tippanna L. P., Co-Founder & Proprietor, M.Sc. Industrial Chemistry, brings professional expertise to our operations.

Together, we are committed to providing responsible pest management with a focus on safety, effectiveness, and customer trust.
